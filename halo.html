Halo Infinite

Synopsis

Halo Infinite is the next chapter in the legendary Halo franchise, developed by 343 Industries. Players step into the armor of Master Chief to face a new threat on the expansive ringworld Zeta Halo.

With a mix of classic Halo gameplay and modern open-world exploration, Halo Infinite delivers intense first-person shooting, a gripping narrative, and a robust multiplayer experience that continues the saga of humanity’s fight against the Banished.
