Horizon Forbidden West

Synopsis

Horizon Forbidden West is an open-world action RPG developed by Guerrilla Games. You play as Aloy, a skilled hunter and archer, as she journeys into the mysterious frontier known as the Forbidden West.

Facing awe-inspiring machines and hostile tribes, Aloy must uncover the secrets behind a spreading blight and save humanity’s future. With breathtaking landscapes, dynamic combat, and a deep narrative, Horizon Forbidden West expands the world introduced in Horizon Zero Dawn.
