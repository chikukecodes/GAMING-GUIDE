Marvel's Spider-Man

Synopsis

Marvel's Spider-Man is an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Players take on the role of Peter Parker, balancing his life as a young adult and his responsibilities as the iconic superhero.

With fluid web-swinging mechanics, dynamic combat, and a heartfelt story, the game immerses players in a richly detailed New York City. Experience thrilling battles against classic villains and explore a narrative that blends heroism with humanity.
