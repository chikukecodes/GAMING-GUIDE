Elden Ring

Synopsis

Elden Ring, developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco, is an open-world action RPG set in the Lands Between. Players take on the role of the Tarnished, exiled beings summoned back to restore the shattered Elden Ring and become the Elden Lord.

With vast landscapes, intricate dungeons, and brutal combat, Elden Ring combines FromSoftware’s signature difficulty with expansive exploration. Guided by lore co-written by George R.R. Martin, the game delivers a dark fantasy epic filled with gods, demigods, and fallen heroes.

System Requirements

Minimum

  • Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 3 3300X
  • Memory: 12 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 580
  • DirectX: Version 12

Recommended

  • OS: Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600X

  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon RX Vega 56
