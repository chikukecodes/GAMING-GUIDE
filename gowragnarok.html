God of War Ragnarök

Synopsis

Embark on an epic journey in God of War Ragnarök, the sequel to the critically acclaimed God of War (2018). Set in the Nine Realms of Norse mythology, Kratos and his son Atreus must prepare for the prophesied Ragnarök — the end of the world. As tensions rise and gods clash, father and son face difficult choices about fate, family, and survival.

Players will experience visceral combat, breathtaking exploration, and a deeply emotional narrative. With new realms to discover, powerful enemies to battle, and mythic allies to encounter, Ragnarök delivers a grand conclusion to the Norse saga of Kratos and Atreus.

System Requirements

Minimum

  • Processor: Intel i5-6600k / AMD Ryzen 5 2400
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD RX 570
  • DirectX: Version 12

Recommended

  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel i7-8700k / AMD Ryzen 5 3600
