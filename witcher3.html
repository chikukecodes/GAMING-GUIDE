The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

Synopsis

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an open-world RPG set in a rich fantasy universe filled with meaningful choices and impactful consequences. You play as Geralt of Rivia, a professional monster hunter searching for his adopted daughter, who is on the run from the mysterious Wild Hunt.

Explore vast lands, engage in deep narrative-driven quests, and battle both human and monstrous foes. With branching storylines, immersive combat, and breathtaking visuals, The Witcher 3 delivers one of the most acclaimed RPG experiences of all time.
